Frequently Asked Questions

What is Ask Quran?

Ask Quran is an AI-powered app that helps you find relevant Quran verses using natural language questions. Simply type a question or topic, and the app will find the most relevant verses from the Quran along with their English translations.

How do I search for verses?

Type your question or topic in the search bar on the home screen. For example, you can ask "What does the Quran say about patience?" or "Verses about forgiveness." The app uses AI to understand your question and find the most relevant verses.

Is the AI Chat feature accurate?

The AI Chat provides responses based on Quranic content and is designed for educational and exploratory purposes. It should not be taken as official religious rulings. We recommend consulting qualified Islamic scholars for specific religious guidance.

How do I manage my subscription?

You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time:

  • iPhone/iPad: Go to Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions > Ask Quran
  • In the app: Go to Settings > Manage Subscription

How do I restore my purchases?

If you've previously subscribed and need to restore your purchase (e.g., after reinstalling the app or switching devices), go to Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchases."

How do I cancel my subscription?

To cancel, open your device's Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions, then select Ask Quran and tap "Cancel Subscription." Your access will continue until the end of the current billing period.

Can I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple through the App Store. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
