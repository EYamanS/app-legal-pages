Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 24, 2026

Ask Quran ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Search queries you enter to find Quran verses
  • Chat messages when using the AI assistant
  • Bookmarked verses you save

Information collected automatically:

  • Anonymous usage analytics (searches, feature usage)
  • Device information (device type, operating system)
  • App performance data

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our Quran search and AI chat features
  • To personalize your experience and save your preferences
  • To analyze app usage and improve our services
  • To process your subscription and manage your account

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. Search history and bookmarks are associated with an anonymous identifier and are not linked to your personal identity unless you create an account.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Database and authentication
  • OpenAI: AI-powered search and chat features
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management
  • AppsFlyer: Attribution and analytics

These services have their own privacy policies governing their use of your data.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Delete your data
  • Opt out of analytics tracking
  • Cancel your subscription at any time

Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
