Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 12, 2026

PhotoSeek ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Photo and video library metadata (filenames, dimensions, dates)
  • AI-generated vector embeddings of your media (photos and videos are NOT stored on our servers)
  • Account information (via Apple or Google Sign-In)
  • Communications with us

Information collected automatically:

  • Anonymous usage analytics and device information
  • Subscription and payment data (processed by Apple/Google, managed by RevenueCat)
  • App performance data

Your Media Privacy

Your photos and videos never leave your device. We only store mathematical representations (embeddings) of your media on our servers for search functionality. These embeddings cannot be used to reconstruct your original photos or videos.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our AI-powered photo and video search features
  • To personalize your experience and save your preferences
  • To analyze app usage and improve our services
  • To process subscriptions and manage your account

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. Only vector embeddings and account data are stored on our servers -- your actual photos and videos remain on your device.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Gemini API: AI embeddings for media search
  • Supabase: Database and authentication
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management
  • AppsFlyer: Install attribution
  • Firebase Analytics: Usage analytics

These services have their own privacy policies governing their use of your data.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Delete your data and embeddings
  • Opt out of analytics tracking
  • Cancel your subscription at any time

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
