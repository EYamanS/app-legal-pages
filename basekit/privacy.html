Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 18, 2026

BaseKit is operated by Glyde Games ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the BaseKit mobile application. BaseKit is designed from the ground up as an offline-first, on-device AI assistant — the vast majority of what you do in the app never leaves your phone.

Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Account information via Apple Sign-In or email/password registration (managed by Supabase Auth)
  • Your subscription purchase (processed by Apple, managed by RevenueCat)
  • Communications you send to us directly (support email)

Information collected automatically:

  • Anonymous product usage events (via PostHog) — screen views, feature interactions, anonymized performance metrics. No chat content, no photos, no location.
  • Anonymous crash reports (via PostHog) — used only to fix bugs.
  • Subscription lifecycle events (via RevenueCat) — purchase, renewal, cancellation.

What Never Leaves Your Device

Chat messages. Every question you ask BaseKit is processed by the Gemma 4 language model running directly on your iPhone. Neither your prompt nor the assistant's response is ever transmitted to any server.

Photos. When you use the Identify feature to photograph a plant, injury, or animal, the image is analyzed on-device using Apple's MobileCLIP-S2 Core ML model. Photos never leave your phone and are not uploaded to any service.

Location. If you grant location permission, BaseKit uses your GPS fix only to tailor answers to your region (local snakes, plants, wildlife; altitude-aware medical advice; the local emergency number). Your coordinates are never transmitted.

Conversation history. Past conversations are stored locally in an encrypted SQLite database on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

How We Use Information That Does Leave the Device

  • To create and manage your BaseKit account
  • To verify and renew your subscription
  • To understand how the app is used in aggregate and fix bugs
  • To respond to your support requests

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy governing its use of your data.

  • Supabase — Authentication, account profile storage, knowledge pack catalog
  • RevenueCat — Subscription management and receipt validation
  • PostHog (EU Cloud) — Anonymous product analytics and crash reports
  • AppsFlyer — Install attribution and basic usage analytics. Collects the device IDFA only when you grant permission via the App Tracking Transparency prompt. Never receives chat content, photos, location, or purchase amounts.
  • Apple — App Store purchase and delivery, Sign in with Apple
  • Hugging Face — Source of the Gemma 4 AI model file downloaded to your device on first launch

App Tracking Transparency

During onboarding we ask for your permission to measure which features of BaseKit people actually use — this helps us decide what to improve next. The iOS App Tracking Transparency system dialog gives you a clear yes/no choice. If you say yes, AppsFlyer receives your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) along with anonymous event names. If you say no or skip, AppsFlyer still logs anonymous events but without the IDFA. You can change your choice any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. We do not share data with advertising networks, do not link your device data across apps or websites, and do not work with data brokers.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Delete your account and all associated data (Settings → Delete Account, or email us)
  • Cancel your subscription at any time via your Apple ID settings
  • Request an export of your account data

Data Retention

Account data is retained while your account is active. When you delete your account, your Supabase profile row and associated records are permanently removed. On-device data (chat history, downloaded models) is removed when you delete the app.

Children's Privacy

BaseKit is rated 17+ and is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Educational Reference, Not Medical Advice

BaseKit provides survival and first-aid information drawn from public-domain field manuals. It is educational reference material, not medical advice, and should never replace professional medical care or emergency services. In a life-threatening situation, contact local emergency services first.
