Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 1, 2026

Claw AI ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Account information (via Apple Sign-In or email registration)
  • Chat messages and instructions you send to your AI workspace
  • Channel connection credentials (WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, Slack) stored encrypted on your dedicated workspace
  • Personality and automation configurations
  • Communications with us

Information collected automatically:

  • Anonymous usage analytics and device information (via PostHog)
  • Install attribution data (via AppsFlyer)
  • Subscription and payment data (processed by Apple, managed by RevenueCat)
  • App performance and session replay data (via PostHog, with sensitive inputs masked)

Your Workspace Privacy

Each user receives a dedicated, isolated AI workspace running on its own cloud container. Your workspace data (chat history, browser sessions, channel connections, plugins) is isolated from other users and is not shared or accessible by anyone else.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate your personal AI workspace
  • To execute chat instructions and automation tasks on your behalf
  • To manage channel connections (WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, Slack)
  • To process subscriptions and manage your account
  • To analyze app usage and improve our services
  • To provide customer support

Data Storage and Security

Your workspace runs in an isolated cloud environment with encrypted storage. Chat messages are stored in encrypted databases. Channel credentials are stored within your isolated workspace container and are not accessible to other users or our team.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Authentication and database
  • AWS (Amazon Web Services): Cloud infrastructure for isolated workspaces
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management
  • PostHog: Usage analytics and session replay
  • AppsFlyer: Install attribution
  • OpenRouter: AI model provider for chat

These services have their own privacy policies governing their use of your data.

App Tracking Transparency

We request your permission before collecting your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for install attribution purposes. You can change this permission at any time in your device's Settings under Privacy > Tracking.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Opt out of analytics tracking
  • Cancel your subscription at any time
  • Request export of your data

Data Retention

Your workspace data is retained while your account is active. When you delete your account, all workspace data, chat history, and channel connections are permanently removed within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

Claw AI is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
