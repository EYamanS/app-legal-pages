Frequently Asked Questions

What is Claw AI?

Claw AI is a personal AI workspace that lives in the cloud. It can browse the web, automate tasks, and connect to your messaging apps (WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, Slack) — all controlled through a simple chat interface.

How does it work?

When you start a session, Claw AI spins up a dedicated cloud workspace just for you. You chat with your AI assistant, and it can browse the internet, run automations, and send messages through your connected channels.

Is my data private?

Yes. Each user gets a completely isolated workspace. Your chat history, browser sessions, and channel connections are not shared with or accessible by other users.

What channels can I connect?

Claw AI supports WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, and Slack. Connect them from the Hub tab to let your AI workspace send and receive messages on your behalf.

What are the free tier limits?

Free users get a limited number of messages per window and a shorter idle timeout. Upgrade to Pro for more messages, always-on workspaces, and access to all channels and plugins.

How do I manage my subscription?

Go to your device's Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions. From there you can view, change, or cancel your subscription.

How do I restore my purchases?

Go to the Settings tab in Claw AI and tap "Restore Purchases". Your subscription status is automatically synced with your Apple ID.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to your device's Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions, tap Claw AI, and tap "Cancel Subscription". Your access will continue until the end of the current billing period.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and select the purchase you'd like to request a refund for.

How do I delete my account?

Go to the Settings tab in Claw AI and tap "Sign Out". To permanently delete your account and all associated data, contact us at yaman@walle.tr.

My workspace isn't responding

Try stopping your session from the Activity tab and starting a new one. If the issue persists, contact us.
