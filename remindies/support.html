Frequently Asked Questions

What is Remindies?

Remindies is an AI-powered reminder app that lets you create and manage reminders using natural language and voice input. Simply type or speak your reminder and the app will automatically parse the date, time, and details for you.

How do I create a reminder?

Tap the "+" tab and type your reminder in natural language (e.g., "Call dentist tomorrow at 3pm"). The app will automatically detect the date, time, and title. You can also adjust the details manually before saving.

How does voice input work?

Voice input is a Premium feature. Tap the microphone button on the add screen, speak your reminder, and the app will transcribe and parse it into a reminder automatically.

How do I manage my subscription?

To manage your Remindies Pro subscription on iPhone or iPad, go to Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions. From there you can view, change, or cancel your subscription.

How do I restore my purchases?

If you've previously purchased Remindies Pro and it's not showing as active, try signing out and signing back in. Your subscription status is automatically synced with your Apple ID.

How do I cancel my subscription?

To cancel, go to your device's Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions, tap Remindies, and tap "Cancel Subscription". Your access will continue until the end of the current billing period.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and select the purchase you'd like to request a refund for.

How is my data used?

We take your privacy seriously. Your reminders are stored securely and voice recordings are only used for transcription. For full details, please read our Privacy Policy.
