Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 28, 2026

Remindies ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Account information (email address via Apple Sign In)
  • Reminders you create (titles, notes, due dates)
  • Voice recordings for transcription (processed and not stored)

Information collected automatically:

  • Anonymous usage analytics
  • Device information (device type, operating system)
  • Push notification tokens
  • App performance data

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide reminder notifications at your scheduled times
  • To sync your reminders across devices
  • To transcribe voice input into text reminders
  • To analyze app usage and improve our services
  • To process subscriptions and manage your account

Data Storage and Security

Your reminders are stored securely using Supabase with industry-standard encryption. Voice recordings are sent to OpenAI for transcription and are not permanently stored.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase - Data storage and authentication
  • OpenAI - Voice transcription and natural language processing
  • RevenueCat - Subscription management
  • AppsFlyer - Analytics and attribution
  • Expo - Push notifications

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Opt out of analytics tracking

Contact us to exercise these rights.

Children's Privacy

Remindies is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
